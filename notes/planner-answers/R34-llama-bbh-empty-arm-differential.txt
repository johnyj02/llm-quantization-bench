There's a fifth option in Q4 that resolves Q1, Q2 and Q4 together, and it costs nothing you care about.

## Apply the terminator at scoring time instead of as a generation-time stop

Generate to EOS or the budget; apply the `until` strings when scoring. **For greedy decoding this yields byte-identical scored text** — the prefix is the same whether you stop at token 2 or truncate there later — so published semantics are preserved exactly, R19.1's constraint is respected, and G2 is untouched (it tests prompts, not `generation_kwargs`).

What it buys: the discarded continuation becomes observable, so you can tell whether an answer existed downstream of the `Q:`. Right now you cannot, and that's the whole problem.

Cost: ~1,100 affected Llama items × ~800 extra tokens ≈ 900k tokens. Trivial. It does bump `decoding_fingerprint()` and invalidate cached cells, which is the real price.

**And it's a generalizable recommendation for the paper**, alongside "report K" and "report item ordering": stop sequences destroy information irreversibly, while scoring-time application costs tokens and preserves everything.

## Q2 — Defect in measurement, and the compliance framework cannot absorb it

Your "finding" case fails on one point: an empty generation at `n_gen_tokens=2` is **not** ABSENT compliance. ABSENT means the model produced no identifiable answer. Here the model was stopped before it could produce anything. Labelling it ABSENT records *our configuration* as a model property — the same category error as calling `TRUNC_STOPSEQ` a competence failure.

The underlying behaviour *is* a finding: Llama under chat templating continues the few-shot transcript at a rate that differs by arm (17.5% bf16 → 3.3% gptq-4bit), plausibly because the more capable arm pattern-matches the transcript more faithfully. Report that rate.

But the measurement defect is real and it is not tolerable, for one specific reason: **the 14 pp differential runs in the direction that flatters our own hypothesis.** That is the same failure mode §4.0 exists to prevent and the same one R10's found/committed leak had. We have already been wrong in this exact shape once.
