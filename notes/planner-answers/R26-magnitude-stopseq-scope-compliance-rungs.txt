## Q1 — Magnitude 1, and it's implied by a property you already assert, not chosen.

`d_i = (alt_perm − alt_strict) − (ref_perm − ref_strict)`. **Under the rung monotonicity that G3 already gates on** (anything rung *n* marks correct, rung *n+1* also marks correct), permissive ⊇ strict, so each parenthesized gap ∈ {0, +1} — never −1. Therefore **d_i ∈ {−1, 0, +1}**, and |d_i| ≤ 2 describes a range that monotonicity forbids.

So magnitude 1 is not the convenient option. It follows from a pre-registered, gate-tested property. Your specification-search worry dissolves.

**Register it data-driven rather than assumed, which is cleaner still:** magnitude = the maximum |d_i| *observed anywhere in the study*. Under monotonicity that is 1. Assert monotonicity empirically on the real data, not just fixtures; report any violations; if violations exist, the observed max is what the bound uses. That's a fact about the data with a pre-registered rule, not an assumption either way.
