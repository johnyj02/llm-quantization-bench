## Enumerate these in the prereg, or the code decides again

(ii) needs its edge cases pinned, and this is the pattern that has already cost four instances:

- **Multiple commitments** → use the first.
- **A commitment that yields no parseable answer** ("the answer is unclear") → fallback applies to the **pre-commitment span only**, not the whole string. Otherwise the rule reaches past a commitment again, which is precisely what's being prevented.

- **Commitment at position 0** → pre-commitment span is empty; extraction falls to the commitment's own span.
