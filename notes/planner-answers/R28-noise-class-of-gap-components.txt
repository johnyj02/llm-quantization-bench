## Better than assigning a class: measure this one's own floor

`recovered` and `destroyed` move whenever an invocation flips one rung but not the other — the same event that moves the gap. The gap is their difference, so if their movements are positively correlated the gap moves *less* than either component. There is no mechanism by which a component moves less than the net, and your measured gap movement of 1.67 pp is therefore a **lower** bound on the components, not an upper one.
