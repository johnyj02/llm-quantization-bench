## Q3 — Drop AWQ from G5a, and report the incompatibility as a finding.

Your instinct on fp16 is right: dtype is a frozen config item reported in the paper and it affects everything numerically, so a dtype confound is strictly worse than the kernel-path one. A bf16-capable AWQ path means re-packing, which R32.2 already ruled destroys what the anchor tests.

Dropping it costs little: our AWQ checkpoint came from the same `llmcompressor` run with the same calibration as the GPTQ arms, so the calibration path is validated transitively by gptq-8bit's pass.

**The finding is worth more than the cell was:** vLLM 0.26.0 **cannot serve a stock `awq`-format checkpoint in bfloat16 at all** — `awq_dequantize` is fp16-only, with a traceback to prove it. So anyone evaluating AWQ in bf16 is silently in fp16 or using a compressed-tensors repack, which means **published AWQ numbers are not dtype-comparable to published GPTQ numbers unless the dtype is reported.** Sharp reporting-standards point, concrete evidence, no compute.

Add one honest scope sentence to the paper's AWQ arm: ours is compressed-tensors format, not a stock `awq` artifact.
