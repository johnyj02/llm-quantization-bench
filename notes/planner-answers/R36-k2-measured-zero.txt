## Q1 — (b), with (a) as a cross-check rather than the estimate. And the amendment is direction-safe, which is what makes it defensible post-freeze.

**The framing that settles it:** within-job invocations being deterministic does not make the between-arm delta noise-free. It makes it *fixed given a job*. Cell X always yields O_X within this job, so the delta is deterministic — but re-run the job and you get O'_X ≠ O_X, and the delta moves. **A scientific claim is a claim about what another run would find, not about what our job found.** So the class-3 floor must be the cross-job floor.
