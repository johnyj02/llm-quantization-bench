## Your four questions, directly

**Q2 is fine and proven.** `TokensPrompt(prompt_token_ids=...)` is already used at `generate.py:842` on the path that has run many times on the GPU, and since it is a TypedDict, `requests[k]["prompt_token_ids"]` is ordinary dict indexing at runtime.

**Q3 is correct, and `enable_prefix_caching=False` is not merely compatible but required**, since vLLM V1 refuses `prompt_logprobs` with prefix caching enabled. `async_scheduling=False` matters for the same reason. I see no batch-invariance interaction: the BI overrides substitute matmul and attention kernels, while `prompt_logprobs` only changes which positions get logits computed. Reading only, not executed, since vllm cannot be imported here. The reassuring part is that the failure mode is fail-closed: if the engine returned no prompt logprobs, `plp` is empty, `lp` is None, and the method raises rather than guessing.

**Q4 is clean.** `build_cells` produces `revision`, `quant_method=None`, `bits=16` and `quant_config_hash=None` for bf16, `main()` injects `base_repo` at line 187, and `validate()` accepts both Nones. I confirmed that by execution when I built and validated a bf16 record.
