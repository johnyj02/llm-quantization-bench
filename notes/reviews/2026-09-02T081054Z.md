## Your five questions

**1. Can the gate report a verdict it has not earned? No, and I checked it exhaustively.**

For a well-formed interval (`ci_low <= ci_high`), `not straddles` is *equivalent* to "the CI lies entirely within `[-2, +2]`". The proof is short: `not straddles` means `|ci_low| <= 2 and |ci_high| <= 2`, i.e. both limits in `[-2, 2]`; and conversely if `ci_low >= -2 and ci_high <= 2` then `ci_low <= ci_high <= 2` gives `|ci_low| <= 2` and `ci_high >= ci_low >= -2` gives `|ci_high| <= 2`. So your symmetric-band intuition is right, and the two-term absolute test is the correct encoding of it.

Your two cases:

| point | CI | `inside` | `straddles` | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| −1.9 | [−3.5, −0.3] | True | True (\|−3.5\|>2) | **INCONCLUSIVE** ✓ |
| 0.0 | [−2.1, +2.1] | True | True (\|−2.1\|>2) | **INCONCLUSIVE** ✓ |

PASS requires `inside and not straddles`, which is exactly "point in band **and** whole CI in band". There is no input combination reaching PASS that should not, because PASS is strictly the conjunction of the two conditions R30 names.

**The one gap is on the FAIL side, and it matches R30 as written, so I am flagging it rather than calling it a defect.** A point of −2.1 with a CI of [−4.0, +0.5] returns FAIL, definitively, even though the interval covers values comfortably inside the band. R30's registered wording is: *"pass on the point estimate, and pre-register that a CI straddling ±2.0 pp is recorded as inconclusive, investigate **rather than a clean pass**."* It addresses only the pass side. So the asymmetry is registered, and the code is faithful to it. But the consequence is that a marginal FAIL blocks main-v1 without the INCONCLUSIVE escape its mirror image gets — which is the "wastes a 17-hour run" half of the risk you named. That is a question for the planner, and it is worth asking before the gate runs rather than after it fails.

One smaller note on the same function: `verdict_for` applies the `straddles` test identically to a bootstrap CI and to a `zero_variance_bound` worst-case bound. R26.1 explicitly withdrew the H6 overlap framing on the grounds that *"a worst-case bound and a bootstrap percentile interval are different objects and must not be read against each other"*, and here they are read against the same criterion. At n=1319 the bound is ±0.29 pp so the outcome is right either way, and the degenerate branch is unlikely on the gate metric anyway since two different quantizations will disagree on some items. Worth a sentence in the report saying which object the verdict was evaluated against — `interval_method` already carries it, so surface it in the printed table.

**2. `paired_difference`: yes, and yes.** `contrib = int(ours[i]) - int(official[i])` over `sorted(set(ours) & set(official))` is `ours − official` as named, and `point = contrib.mean() * 100` is the paired mean difference in points. The bootstrap resamples item indices and averages `contrib`, so each item's pair of outcomes moves together — that is a correct paired cluster bootstrap, and it is the construction that makes the SE reflect discordance rather than the marginals, which is what your n=1319 argument depends on. Reporting `n_discordant` alongside is the right instinct, since it is what a reader needs to check the resolution claim.

**Magnitude 1 is correct, and hardcoding it here is correct.** `contrib` is a difference of two per-item **indicators**, each in {0,1}, so it lies in {−1, 0, +1} and `|contrib| <= 1` is a *structural* bound that no data can violate. That is different from `interaction_marginal`'s difference of *differences*, where the bound depends on rung monotonicity and therefore had to become observed after R26.1/R27 showed monotonicity fails. So the two call sites should not be made consistent with each other: deriving is right there, hardcoding is right here. I mention it because "make it consistent" is the tempting next change and it would be wrong.

The pairing key: your reasoning is right today, and the precondition is unasserted.** Keying `scored[(task, rung, arm, side)][item_id]` is safe here because the stage is single-model (`ANCHOR_MODEL`), `gsm8k` and `gsm8k_cot` declare no `subtasks` in `configs/tasks.yaml`, and there is one `budget_level` and one `prompt_variant`.
